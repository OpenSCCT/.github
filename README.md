# OpenSCCT

Community projects for **Tom Clancy’s Splinter Cell Chaos Theory**. Start with the
[project hub](https://master.lumbridge.co.uk/) or [download the launcher](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT/releases/latest).

## Originals

| Edition | Play | Development |
|---|---|---|
| [PlayStation 2](https://master.lumbridge.co.uk/projects/ps2) | Load your own Chaos Theory ISO with PCSX2. Keep original and patched disc images side by side. | [PS2 executable research](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT/tree/main/decomp/ps2) |
| [Original PC / Steam](https://master.lumbridge.co.uk/projects/pc) | Start the campaign, co-op or Versus from your installation. Enhanced and Reloaded are optional Versus profiles. | [PC executable research](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT/tree/main/decomp/pc) |

## Modernisations

These two Spies vs. Mercenaries clients use new engines, graphics and features.

| Edition | Play | Development |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenSCCT Godot](https://master.lumbridge.co.uk/projects/godot) | Build the starting content locally from your Steam installation of Chaos Theory. Its pre-installed Versus maps form the starting roster. Add community-created maps as optional downloads. | [Godot client](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT/tree/main/game) and [shared simulation](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT/tree/main/src) |
| [OpenSCCT Unreal](https://master.lumbridge.co.uk/projects/unreal) | Play on Windows with Unreal Engine 5 lighting, materials and effects. Prepare its stock Versus maps and shared game files locally from your Steam installation. | [Unreal client and bridge](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT-Unreal) |

## Follow development

[Per-executable progress](https://master.lumbridge.co.uk/progress) tracks original
builds and verified matching code. [Release notes](https://master.lumbridge.co.uk/changelog)
cover playable builds. The [core repository](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT)
contains the launchers, website, shared simulation, Godot client and research tools.

## Create and play maps

- Build a custom Versus map and try it in either engine with [OpenSCCT Editor](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT-Editor). The editor uses the locally prepared Steam files. The [community map catalog](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT-Content) and [Unreal map packaging tools](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/OpenSCCT-Unreal-Content) describe optional downloads.
- For original PC Versus, use the [Reloaded Editor fork](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/SCCT_Versus_Reloaded_Editor), [Map Manager](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/SCCT-Map-Manager) and [community map collection](https://github.com/OpenSCCT/SCCT-Maps).
- See the upstream [Enhanced SCCT Versus releases](https://github.com/Joshhhuaaa/EnhancedSCCTVersus/releases) and [Versus Reloaded features](https://allypal.github.io/SCCT_Versus_Reloaded/features/) for changes to the original multiplayer game.
